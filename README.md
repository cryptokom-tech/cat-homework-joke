Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands"](https://make.fun/c/u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262): one joke about cats, a single line under 200 characters, judged pairwise by an AI judge until one joke is left standing. Winner takes the $2 prize.

## The joke

> My cat sat on my homework when she was a kitten. Twelve years later, she's still sitting on my homework. I'm 34.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 112 characters — and self-checks the 1–200 character gate before exiting. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the local judging of all 16 candidate lines.
