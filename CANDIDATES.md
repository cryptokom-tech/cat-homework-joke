# Local judging — 16 candidate one-liners

Judge brief criteria, in order: (1) one-line cat joke with a setup and a turn
that lands; (2) laugh out loud on first read, not merely a smile at
cleverness; (3) surprise / misdirection / fresh angle, not a worn pun;
(4) tight, no padding, no explanation after the punchline; (5) retellable
to a friend the same day. All candidates are original lines written in this
session. Field scan: public API shows submission titles only (no entry text
was copied); rival topics seen — gravity/coffee spill, puzzle feeder, will
leaves everything to the dog, $90 bed, empty corner, can't hunt. Our lines
avoid those topics.

## The pick

**A — "My cat sat on my homework when she was a kitten. Twelve years later,
she's still sitting on my homework. I'm 34." (112 chars)**

Three-beat arc: kitten habit → twelve years unchanged → the turn is the
human, not the cat ("I'm 34" — the adult still has homework, and the cat
still sits on it). The surprise is a time jump, not a pun; the last two
words do all the work; nothing is cuttable. Most retellable of the set.

## Runners-up

- **B — "My cat learned to open doors. I changed the locks. She learned to
  open the new locks. I now live in her house." (110 chars)** — clean
  escalation, strong closer, but "now it's the cat's house" is a familiar
  trope; loses to A on freshness (criterion 3).
- **C — "I left my cat alone for a weekend with an automatic feeder. She
  has filed a formal complaint. It is written on my couch." (120 chars)**
  — visual payoff, specific; "written on my couch" lands well, but the
  setup is longer and the laugh is a smile more than a laugh-out-loud.
- **D — "I bought a baby monitor to check on my cat while I'm out. She
  stares into it for hours. I think she's checking on me." (117 chars)**
  — neat reversal, but "the pet watches us back" is a known angle.
- **E — "My cat brings me dead mice as gifts, which is sweet, except I live
  on the fourteenth floor and she has never been outside." (122 chars)**
  — biggest surprise of the set, but the punchline needs a beat of thought
  (where do the mice come from?) and the brief warns a joke that has to be
  worked out has already lost.

## The rest (cut)

- F "The vet said my cat is overweight. My cat said the vet is underfed.
  I believe the cat." — quick, but the turn is just contrarianism.
- G "My cat knocked a glass off the counter, then sat down and watched me
  clean it up like a documentary about human failure." — good image, but
  knocking things off counters is the most common cat-joke stock.
- H "I asked my cat for one quiet morning. She brought me a sock at 4 a.m.
  and screamed until I thanked her." — specific, but the turn is thin.
- I "My cat only drinks from my glass. I bought her a fountain. She drinks
  from my glass. The fountain drinks from my wallet." — repetition joke,
  third beat is the weakest.
- J "I trained my cat to come when called. She comes when called — to watch
  me realize she was never going to come." — clever, not funny enough.
- K "My cat's favourite toy is a $2 spring. Her second favourite is my $400
  headphones. Her third is my will to live." — list joke, no real turn.
- L "I told my cat she's adopted. She said that explains the food, the
  service, and why she chose this house anyway." — dialogue joke, flat
  ending.
- M "My cat sleeps 16 hours a day and still acts like I owe her rent.
  Technically, she owes me rent. She knows. She doesn't care." — rent
  angle already used in our own earlier bounty entry; no fresh turn.
- N "I put my cat on a diet. She put me on a guilt trip. The guilt trip has
  no return ticket." — wordplay, and "guilt trip" is a worn pun.
- O "My cat watches bird videos on my tablet. Yesterday she tried to swipe
  to the next bird. I have raised a streamer." — topical, but the last line
  explains the joke.
- P "I spent $200 on a cat tree. My cat sleeps on the router. The router is
  warm. The router is also now hers." — decent escalation, but overlaps the
  rival "$90 bed" topic and the ending is soft.

## Verdict

A wins locally: best turn (time jump + "I'm 34"), freshest angle, tightest
at 112 chars, most retellable. Submit A.
